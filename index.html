QA • 40-Min Clock Hard Cutoff: 120s in R1

QA Three-Round Scanning Protocol

ROUND 1: SITTER EXTRACTION Mins 0 – 18

Scan all 22 Qs rapidly. Immediately solve 6–8 single-concept sitters (<90s each). Bookmark moderate multi-step problems. Ruthlessly skip walls of text.

ROUND 2: BOOKMARK CONVERSION Mins 18 – 34

Return to bookmarked problems and solve 5–7 moderate questions using structural shortcuts (center bisection, LCM work units, $n=1,2,3$ substitution).

ROUND 3: TITA SWEEP & 50/50 SPLITS Mins 34 – 40

Mark any 50/50 two-option MCQ split ($EV = +1.0$) and key in an educated numerical response for 100% of the ~8 zero-penalty TITA questions.

DILR • 40-Min Clock Abandon Trigger: Min 4.0

DILR 5-Min Triage & Abandonment

PHASE 1: 5-MINUTE TRIAGE SCAN Mins 0 – 5

Inspect all 5 sets without solving. Rank top 3 sets by: (1) Fixed Structural Base (Charts, Tables, Matrix, Venn), (2) Anchor Clue Density, and (3) Closed MCQ Stems.

PHASE 2: INVARIANT & GROUP LOCK Set Mins 0 – 4

Extract global match/sum invariants first. Lock group splits before filling round schedules. Grab immediate 1–2 partial-deduction questions (+3 to +6M).

HARD ABANDONMENT TRIGGER At Minute 4.0

If the master structural frame is not locked with $\ge 2$ fixed anchors (or $\le 2$ sub-cases) by Minute 4.0, abandon immediately to prevent sunk-cost bleed.

VARC • 40-Min Clock 27m RC + 13m VA

VARC 2-Engine Pacing & Elimination

ENGINE 1: RC DISCOURSE MAPPING Mins 0 – 27

Spend 6.5–7m per passage (for 4 RCs) or 8.5m (for 3 RCs, skipping toughest). Track thesis, paragraph roles, and shifts between cited scholars and the author.

3-FLAW DISTRACTOR AUDIT Per RC / Summary

Never look for the "right-sounding" choice. Eliminate 3 options via: Quantifier Inflation (*may $\rightarrow$ must*), Scope Distortion, or Polarity Inversion.

ENGINE 2: 8-QUESTION VA SPRINT Mins 27 – 40

~90s per VA item. Use Bidirectional Hinge Checks on Sentence Placement and Cause + Effect decomposition on Para Summary.

Level 1: Direct (Diff 3–4) Target: 60–75s

CAT 2024 Slot 1: Fruit Seller Ratio

"In a basket of 187 fruits, apples and mangoes are in ratio 5:2. After selling 75 apples, 26 mangoes, and half the oranges, unsold apples to unsold oranges are in ratio 3:2. Find total unsold fruits."

Execution: Let initial apples $= 5x$, mangoes $= 2x$, unsold oranges $= y$ (initial oranges $= 2y$). Since $(5x - 75)/y = 3/2 \Rightarrow y = \frac{2}{3}(5x - 75)$.

Substitute into total $7x + 2y = 187 \Rightarrow 41x = 861 \Rightarrow x = 21$. Unsold fruits evaluate to 66.

⚠️ Common Error: Computing sold fruits (121) instead of unsold (66).

Level 2: Applied (Diff 5–6) Target: 90–120s

CAT 2024: Renu & Seema Work Shift

"Renu takes 15 days (4 hrs/day) to finish a job; Seema takes 8 days (5 hrs/day). Together, Seema works double the hrs/day of Renu, while Renu works double the days of Seema. If Renu works 2 hrs/day, how many days does Seema work?"

Execution: Renu $= 60$ man-hrs; Seema $= 40$ man-hrs. Set $W = \text{LCM}(60, 40) = 120$ units $\Rightarrow r_R = 2\text{ u/hr}, r_S = 3\text{ u/hr}$.

Seema works $d$ days at $4\text{ hrs/day}$; Renu works $2d$ days at $2\text{ hrs/day}$. Total work: $d(4)(3) + (2d)(2)(2) = 120 \Rightarrow 20d = 120 \Rightarrow \mathbf{d = 6\text{ days}}$.

⚠️ Common Error: Reversing the ratio of days vs. hours/day.

Level 3: Integrated (Diff 7–8) Target: 110–140s

CAT 2024 Slot 1: Coordinate Annulus Area

"Find the area in the XY-plane bounded by $y \ge x + 4$ and $-4 \le x^2 + y^2 + 4(x - y) \le 0$."

Execution: Complete squares: $4 \le (x+2)^2 + (y-2)^2 \le 8$. This is an annulus centered at $(-2, 2)$ with $r_1 = 2, r_2 = \sqrt{8}$ and total area $\pi(8 - 4) = 4\pi$.

Substitute center $(-2, 2)$ into $y = x + 4 \Rightarrow 2 = -2 + 4$. The line passes through the center and bisects the annulus! Area $= \frac{1}{2}(4\pi) = \mathbf{2\pi}$.

✅ Rule: Attempt immediately if center-bisection is spotted; skip if integrating chords.

Step 1 • Minutes 0 to 2

Global Structure & Invariant Check

In the CAT 2024 Slot 1 QUIET Tournament (6 teams in two groups of 3; intra-group once, cross-group twice), each team plays $2 + 2(3) = 8$ games $\Rightarrow (6\times 8)/2 = 24$ total games across 8 rounds of 3 games each. In CAT 2021 Chango (12 players, 4 groups of 3 + KO), $8 + 2 + 1 = 11$ total games (22 player-appearances).

Step 2 • Minutes 2 to 4

Group Partitioning via Frequency Clues

Use repeat-pairing clues to lock groups before touching round schedules: any pair meeting twice must belong to different groups; any pair meeting once must belong to the same group. Once $\{1, 5, 6\}$ and $\{2, 3, 4\}$ are partitioned, Question 3 ("Odd team out among 2, 3, 4, 5") is solved immediately for +3 marks.

Step 3 • Minute 4.0 Gate

Abandonment Decision Trigger

Candidates who attempt to fill Rounds 1–8 directly without fixing the group partition branch into dozens of incompatible permutations. Rule: If your master structural frame is not locked with $\ge 2$ fixed anchors by Minute 4.0, pivot immediately to your next ranked set.

Sentence Placement (Diff 5–6) Target: 75–90s

CAT 2024 Slot 1: Renaissance Polyphonic Music

Target Sentence: "Comprehending a wide range of emotions, Renaissance music nevertheless portrayed all emotions in a balanced and moderate fashion."

Bidirectional Hinge Check: Blanks (1) and (2) discuss the 1588 London publication of Italian madrigals. Blank (3) sits right after texture and immediately before: "Extreme use of and contrasts in dynamics, rhythm, and tone colour do not occur." This sentence directly elaborates on "balanced and moderate fashion", locking Option (3) with 100% certainty.

Para Summary (Diff 6–7) Target: 90–110s

CAT 2024 Slot 1: Cartography Usability (43.86% Error Rate)

Passage contrasts past mapmakers (minimal usability research) with modern mapmakers (who must evaluate usability because technological developments made readers demanding).

Cause + Effect Audit: Cause = technological developments making readers demanding; Core Effect = cartographers must focus on map usability. Eliminate Option 1 (narrow "innovations"), Option 2 (fabricates "series of experiments"), and Option 4 (drops technology cause), locking Option 3.
